START HERE
==========

1. Unzip the folder first
   Extract the whole zip before you open anything (Windows: right-click it and
   choose Extract All; macOS: double-click it). Keep the folders as they are:
   the pages link to each other and to the assets folder.

2. Open the course home
   Open index.html in the extracted folder. It has the schedule and a link to
   every week's slides, playground and lab, and to the term project.

3. In week 1: set up PostgreSQL
   Lab 0 (weeks/week01/lab0.html) walks you through installing PostgreSQL on
   your own computer and loading the course database. There is no university
   lab: all work happens on your computer. Lab 0 also offers a Google Colab
   path, but from Lab 8 in week 13 you need PostgreSQL installed on your
   computer, so installing it now saves trouble later.

4. Every week
   Read the slides (weeks/weekNN/index.html), try the week's playground, then
   do the lab. Face-to-face class: Tuesdays, 3:00 to 5:40 p.m. CT at the
   Missouri Innovation Campus. Online section: the recording and the weekly
   quiz are in Brightspace; take the quiz within 48 hours of the recording.

5. Submitting labs
   Upload one zip file per lab to that lab's assignment in Brightspace, named
   labN_lastname_firstname.zip (for example lab0_rivera_jamie.zip). Each lab
   page lists the files to put in it; most labs come with a labN_answers.txt
   to fill in. Upload again to fix something before the deadline: the latest
   zip is graded.

6. Deadlines
   Sunday, 11:59 p.m. CT, unless the assignment says otherwise. Labs and
   project milestones lose 10% per day for up to three days, then are not
   accepted. Quizzes and exams are never late.

7. Exams
   Midterm on Tuesday, March 2 (weeks 1 to 7), with a practice midterm in
   weeks/week08/practice.html. Comprehensive final in finals week, May 3 to 7.
   Online students take both in Brightspace.

8. Graduate students (CS 5200)
   Handouts mark the extra parts for you with the label Graduate (CS 5200).
   The exams have one extra question, G1.

Running the SQL playgrounds
---------------------------
The SQL playgrounds (weeks/weekNN/playground.html) run PostgreSQL inside your
browser. Browsers will not run them from a double-clicked file, so serve the
folder first. Open a terminal in the extracted cs5200-spring2027 folder and
run:

    python3 -m http.server 8000

On Windows, if python3 is not found, use: py -m http.server 8000. Then open
http://localhost:8000/ in your browser and click through to the playground.
Leave the terminal open while you use it, and press Ctrl+C to stop. The first
load needs an internet connection. Slides, labs and project pages open with a
plain double-click; only the playgrounds need this.

Using the slides
----------------
In the slides, use the arrow keys or the Previous and Next buttons to move.
Press O, or the Slides button, to see every slide at once and jump to any of
them. R replays a slide's animation and P prints or saves as PDF. On a phone,
turn it sideways for larger slides.
